How it works

  1. 1

    Paste your list

    Copy it from a text, an email, a takeoff or a spreadsheet. QuoteRunner turns it into clean lines with quantities, units and part numbers.

  2. 2

    Suppliers answer their way

    Each supplier gets an email with a quote link. They type prices on their phone, or reply with their usual PDF. Slow ones get a reminder before the deadline.

  3. 3

    Award and send POs

    One table shows every price, lead time and freight charge. Pick the cheapest supplier per line or overall, then email the purchase orders in a click.

Reads quotes from email and PDF

Forwarded replies and attached PDFs are read into the comparison automatically. You check the prices before anything counts.

Freight-aware split awards

Splitting an order only pays when the savings beat the extra delivery charge. QuoteRunner does that math for you.

Need-by dates that matter

Bids whose lead time misses your date are flagged, and can be left out of the award with one switch.

What you paid last time

Every quote builds your price history, so you see last-paid prices while you write the next request.

Phone quotes count too

Still calling your rep? Type the numbers in and they sit in the same table as everyone else's.

Private by design

Suppliers only ever see your request and their own prices. Never each other's.

Questions

Do my suppliers need an account?

No. They get an email with a private link and enter prices in a couple of minutes, or simply reply with their usual quote.

What if a supplier only sends a PDF?

Forward it or upload it. QuoteRunner reads the prices, lead times and freight into your comparison and highlights anything it isn't sure about.

Can suppliers see each other's prices?

Never. Each supplier only sees your request and their own answer.

Does it work for machine shops and maintenance teams?

Yes. Bar stock, plate, tooling, fasteners and MRO supplies work the same way. Part numbers are kept, and you can mark lines where only the exact item will do.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month to month, and you keep the free plan and your history if you cancel.
