# QuoteRunner — marketing plan

The goal for the first 90 days is **conversations and real usage, not traffic.** Every channel
below is judged by how many buyers it puts on a call or into a first request.

## Positioning

> For owners and buyers at small contractors and job shops who get supplier quotes by phone
> and email, **QuoteRunner** is the purchasing assistant that sends one list to every supplier
> and shows the cheapest way to buy. Unlike enterprise procurement software, it takes ten
> minutes to set up and suppliers need no account.

Three messages, in this order:

1. **Every supplier's price, side by side.** (the outcome)
2. **Suppliers answer the way they already do:** a link, an email reply, or their PDF. (the
   objection "my suppliers won't use another portal")
3. **It shows what you saved.** (the reason to keep paying)

Words to use: *quote, supplier, material list, price, freight, lead time, PO*.
Words to avoid: *procurement, sourcing event, RFx, AI-powered, platform*. Mention AI only where
it does something concrete ("reads the PDF quote for you").

## Ideal first customers

Start with **one trade in one region**: electrical contractors with 5–50 employees in one or
two metro areas. Electrical has standard part numbers (Square D, Eaton, Southwire) and many
competing supply houses, which makes the price comparison obvious. Add plumbing/HVAC next,
then machine shops.

## Channels, in priority order

### 1. Founder-led outreach (days 1–60)

Build a list of 200 electrical contractors from Google Maps, the state licensing database and
association member directories. Record: company, owner name, size, website, phone.

- **Cold email** (20 a day, personal, plain text). Template below.
- **Phone**: call the office manager mid-morning; ask who handles ordering.
- **LinkedIn**: owners and PMs at those companies.

Goal: 15 conversations, 5 pilots. Offer every pilot a 30-day free Pro account and a 20-minute
setup call where you import their suppliers with them.

Cold email:

> Subject: supplier quotes at {Company}
>
> Hi {First name},
>
> Quick question: when you need a material list priced, how many supply houses do you call?
>
> I built a small tool that sends the list to all of them at once. They answer on a link or
> just reply to the email, and you get every price side by side with the cheapest
> combination highlighted, freight included. Suppliers don't need an account.
>
> Would you try it on your next order? It's free, and I'll set it up with you in 15 minutes.
>
> {Your name}

Call opener: "I'm building a tool for contractors that collects supplier quotes in one place.
I'm not selling anything yet. Could I ask how you get prices for a job today?"

Questions for every conversation (write the answers down):

1. Walk me through the last time you priced a material list. Who did it, how long did it take?
2. How many suppliers did you ask? How did they answer?
3. How did you decide who to buy from?
4. What would make you trust a tool with this? What would stop you?
5. What do you pay for software today (estimating, accounting, field apps)?

### 2. The free tool and search (start day 1, compounds slowly)

The **material list cleaner** (`/tools/rfq-builder`) answers searches people make before they
need QuoteRunner, and needs no signup. Add one page per high-intent search:

| Page | Search intent |
|---|---|
| Material list cleaner (live) | "material list template", "convert material list to spreadsheet" |
| RFQ email template for contractors | "request for quote email to supplier" |
| Purchase order template (free PDF from the app's PO page) | "purchase order template construction" |
| How to compare supplier quotes, with freight | "compare supplier quotes spreadsheet" |
| Electrical rough-in material list (by job type) | "electrical rough in material list" |

Each page: answer the question fully, then one call to action ("send this list to your
suppliers in one go"). Measure: tool uses, signups from tool pages.

### 3. Trade communities (from day 15, give before you ask)

- Associations: **IEC** and **NECA** chapters (electrical), **PHCC** (plumbing/HVAC), **ACCA**
  (HVAC), **ABC** (merit-shop contractors), **NTMA** and **PMA** (machine and metal shops).
  Chapter meetings and newsletters take sponsor or speaker slots; a 10-minute talk on "what
  we learned from comparing 100 supplier quotes" (once you have the data) beats an ad.
- Online: contractor Facebook groups, r/electricians, r/Construction, r/HVAC, r/Machinists,
  and trade forums. Read each group's rules. Post useful things (the free tool, the templates,
  real price spreads with names removed), never a sales pitch.

### 4. Short demo videos (from day 15)

60-second screen recordings for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and LinkedIn:

- "I sent one material list to 3 supply houses. Here's what came back." (show the compare
  table)
- "Your supplier sent a PDF quote. Watch it turn into a price table." (AI reading a PDF)
- "Split the order or buy from one supplier? Freight decides." (the split vs. single cards)

### 5. The built-in loop (always on)

Every request reaches 3–10 suppliers. The quote page footer says who sent it and links to a
free account ("Send your own requests for free"). Suppliers' counter staff talk to dozens of
contractors a week. Measure signups from the footer link (`/signup` referrals from `/q/` pages)
before counting on this loop.

Inside a customer, the Team plan grows by inviting PMs and foremen who request materials.

### 6. Partners (from month 3)

- Bookkeepers and fractional CFOs who serve contractors (they see the materials line every
  month).
- Contractor business coaches and peer groups.
- Estimating and takeoff tool vendors (an export into QuoteRunner is a natural next step after
  a takeoff).

Offer partners 20% of the first year's revenue for referred customers.

### 7. Paid search (only after activation works)

Small tests ($300–500) on high-intent searches such as "rfq software for contractors" and
"supplier quote comparison". Turn off anything above $300 per activated account.

## The offer

- **Free plan** that is useful on its own (3 requests a month).
- **Founder pilot:** 30 days of Pro, free, with a setup call.
- **Founding customer price:** $99/month for life for the first 10 paying customers, in
  exchange for a case study.
- **Proof in every account:** the overview shows money saved against the average quote. Ask
  happy pilots for a quote with their number in it.

## First 30 days, week by week

| Week | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Deploy on a domain, set up email (Resend) and inbound email. Build the 200-contractor list. Record the three demo videos. |
| 2 | Send 100 cold emails, make 30 calls. Publish the RFQ email template page. |
| 3 | 5+ conversations. Onboard the first pilots personally. Join 2 association chapters / groups. |
| 4 | Follow up with every pilot after their first request. Write down every request and objection. Publish the "compare supplier quotes" page. |
